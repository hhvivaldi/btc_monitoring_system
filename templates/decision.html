How is the recommendation calculated?

The system uses a normalized and weighted scoring model based on global and BTC-specific indicators:

  1. Normalization: Each indicator is normalized to the range [-1, +1] using a specific scale for comparability (e.g., ±5% change → ±1, funding rate ±0.1% → ±1, basis ±0.5% → ±1, book pressure already in [-1, +1]).
  2. Global Market Score: Nasdaq, S&P 500, VIX (inverted), DXY (inverted), Gold, and Crude Oil are normalized and averaged to form the global score.
  3. BTC-Specific Score: Funding rate, open interest (Δ 1h), basis (perp vs spot), and book pressure are normalized and combined with specific weights to form the BTC score.
  4. Weighting: Global indicators (weight 1 each), funding (2.0), open interest (1.5), basis (1.5), book pressure (2.0).
  5. Total Score: The sum of the global score and BTC-specific score gives the total score.
  6. Decision Thresholds: If total score ≥ +0.5 → BUY. If ≤ -0.5 → SELL. Otherwise → HOLD.
  7. BTC Trend Validation: If the decision is BUY but BTC 1h trend is < -0.2%, or SELL but BTC 1h trend is > +0.2%, the decision is converted to HOLD to avoid signals against immediate price action.
  8. Final Recommendation: The final output is BUY, SELL, or HOLD, with the total score for transparency.
